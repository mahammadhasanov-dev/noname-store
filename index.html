Refund & Return Policy

We want you to be happy with anything you buy from us. If a piece isn't right, you can return it within 30 days of the delivery date for a refund, subject to the conditions below.

  • Returns must be requested within 30 days of the delivery date.

Non-returnable items

We can't accept returns on items that have been worn, washed, or altered; return requests made more than 30 days after delivery; or items marked as final sale at checkout.

How to start a return

Email with your order number, the item(s), and the reason for the return. We'll confirm your return is eligible and send instructions for sending it back.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll let you know whether it's approved. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method via Stripe within 5–10 business days. If an item arrives damaged or defective, we'll exchange it for the same product at no extra cost — just contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo and your order number.

International returns

Customers outside Canada are responsible for return shipping costs and any customs fees incurred when sending an item back to us.

Shipping Policy

Every order is made and packed by our small team before it ships.
